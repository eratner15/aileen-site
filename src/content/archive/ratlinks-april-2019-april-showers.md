## 2-XTREME

Years ago, my two younger brothers screwed a skateboard to a sled, building what they called the “luge cart.”   My brothers proceeded to ride the luge cart downhill daily. I can count the number of times, on one hand, that I ventured into the luge cart and down the hill. Instead, preferring to stay indoors like a house cat. 

My brothers trained hard, sacrificing their bodies, but never qualified for the X-Games. 

My indoor training was masterful, leading to a gold medal in quarantining.

### DO YOU KNOW HOW TO USE THE THREE SEASHELLS?

The COVID-19 crisis and the potential outcomes are shocking to most, but it turns out some people are more prepared than others, thanks to a two-decade-old action film. 

The 1993 movie **Demolition Man **starring, Sandra Bullock, Sylvester Stallone, Wesley Snipes, Benjamin Bratt, and Rob Schnieder, best exemplifies the coronavirus crisis we face today.
