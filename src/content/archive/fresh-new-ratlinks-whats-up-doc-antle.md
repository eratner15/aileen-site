### LUCKY

**“Doc” Antle **was born in Phoenix and grew up on his parent’s ranch in Southern California. Due to difficulty pronouncing his unusual name, he went by “Kevin” in school.  
> 
> His father was a boxer and put him through martial arts training.
> 
> His mother, who nurtured an interest in Eastern philosophy and chose her son’s Hindu name, sometimes cared for sick horses and cows in the family kitchen. “Some blend between Rambo and the Dalai Lama – that’s what I wanted to be when I grew up,” he says.
> 
> Antle spent much of his youth competing in rodeos and training dogs, and didn’t care much for school; he dropped out before the ninth grade. “In my early days,” he says, “drugs, sex, and rock ‘n’ roll were it.”   ([Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-features/tiger-king-doc-antle-zoo-myrtle-beach-70838/))
> 
After dropping out of high school, “Doc” studied abroad in China where Antle earned a doctoral degree in Chinese Medicine after years in the field. 

In 1982, Doc was approached by the Exxon Corporation about giving a presentation with a tiger at the Exxon Annual Convention. By 1983, Doc was training tigers for Exxon’s “Put A Tiger In Your Tank” advertising campaign.   

Raising exotic animals brought Antle more than his fair share of the limelight. 

In 1994, Antle was hired to work on a remake of *The Jungle Book*; then *Ace Ventura: When Nature Calls* and *Mighty Joe Young*. This work earned him enough to buy his Myrtle Beach property, which had been a multifamily horse ranch. 

Today, that property is known as TIGERS / Myrtle Beach Safari. 

At TIGERS, tours are offered three times a week during the summer and a cost of $339 per person. Personal photos and videos are forbidden and professional photos start at $150. According to longtime staffer Rob Johnson, “*If you [take photos], you will be ejected from the park – with a catapult. We’ve got one sitting right back there*.”

### GIMME MORE

Antle breeds tigers for cub petting at his zoo, but can’t house and feed all the cats when they mature. Instead, Antle distributes the animals to disreputable zoos all over America. One named Tony ended up in Louisana at the world-famous “[Tiger Truck Stop](http://www.tigertruckstop.com/)”. 

Whispers of animal trafficking lead South Carolina State law enforcement to serve Antle with a search warrant in December 2019. 

Don’t take my word for it, instead, listen to fellow wildlife enthusiast, Carol Baskin, describe that Federal Agents were “*mad cause Doc was flagrant, even tapping his cell and the phone in the basement*.”

### ME AGAINST THE MUSIC

Just like Britney Spears, Antle was a mainstay in the 90s pop music scene. 

Antle remained visible thanks to his menagerie. 

His elephant Bubbles was featured in two different music videos: “*Together Again*” by **Janet Jackson** and “*Rock Wit U*” by **Ashanti. **

One of Antle’s white tigers featured prominently in “*Hate Me Now*” by **Nas **and** Puff Daddy** where said tiger famously tried to eat Puffy’s white fur coat. 

To keep the media interested and elongate his 15 minutes of fame. The good doctor once took two of his apes to a Carmike Cinema in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, for a public showing of *Dawn of the Planet of the Apes*.

Perhaps saying something about the current state of cinema, Dr. Antle [told the U.K. ](http://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-2691748/Dont-ideas-Safari-park-boss-takes-two-young-chimps-Planet-Apes-movie-says-follow-plot-bark-bad-guys.html#ixzz37Y4BmH7z)*[Daily Mail](http://www.dailymail.co.uk/news/article-2691748/Dont-ideas-Safari-park-boss-takes-two-young-chimps-Planet-Apes-movie-says-follow-plot-bark-bad-guys.html#ixzz37Y4BmH7z)* that the chimps were fully aware of the movie.
> 
> The older one, Vali, is a very bright guy, he’s watched *The Lord of the Rings* many times, and he could follow the whole plot of the movie.
> 
> Vali, the chimp also has a complete connection to the Geico gecko, Antle says.

> “He doesn’t like Flo, from Progressive, particularly.
