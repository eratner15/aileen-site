## **Preamble**

It’s April already.

Make sure you have an umbrella. April is famously known for its showers, which eventually bring May flowers.

In other news, if you are unaware. I am a white male.

To prove this statement, I went to a Weezer concert this month.

If you have never heard of Weezer, they are an alternative rock band formed in 1993 and their recent hit “[Can’t Knock The Hustle”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?utm_campaign=RatLinks%20&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter&v=UV5F3vcQyrY) made the New York Times Magazine *[25 Songs That Matter In 2019](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2019/03/07/magazine/top-songs.html?utm_campaign=RatLinks%20&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter#/weezer)*.

In 2018, Weezer released a cover of Toto’s Africa, which reached #1 on the Billboard Alternative charts and was the first #1 hit for Weezer, since 2008. Over the summer, I was eating lunch at La Esquina, a taqueria and overheard a group next to me discussing this song. One woman said “I like it for karaoke, but it’s just so white.” I really didn’t know what she was talking about until I heard Weezer play the song live. As 30,000 people sung along, I realized that random lady was right. Toto Africa appeared to be peak whiteness, or should I say was peak until Weezer played an acoustic cover of TLC’s “No Scrubs”.

To further prove the above statement, I watched [Camden Riviere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camden_Riviere?utm_campaign=RatLinks%20&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) the world’s #1 [court tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_tennis?utm_campaign=RatLinks%20&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter) player (*a bizarre indoor version of tennis*) play a doubles match. I have never played court tennis and don’t think I am going to start, but if you are a member of a country club looking for sub-par golfers or outdoor tennis players. Let me know.

**Doomsday is coming for New Jersey rail riders.**

The Hudson River Tunnel the connects almost all rail traffic between NJ & NY is crumbling. If just one of the tunnels goes down, traffic would go from 24 trains an hour to six

The Hudson River Tunnel connects 20% of America’s GDP. If it couldn’t run, America would lose $100 million every day, according to the Northeast Corridor Commission. At the broadest level, the national economy would lose $16 billion over a four-year span, equivalent to the loss of 33,000 jobs a year. If you are a New Jersey homeowner, a tunnel failure will cost the state $22 billion in property values.

Beginning in the late 1940s, the [Karen people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karen_people?utm_campaign=RatLinks%20&utm_medium=email&utm_source=Revue%20newsletter#Karen_diaspora), who comprise 7% of 5 million total people in Burma, and the Burmese army were at war. In the early 1990s, the Burmese army launched a major operation to secure the route of an oil pipeline through the area.

In March 1997, a local pastor brought the two illiterate nine-year-olds, twin brothers Johnny and Luther Htoo (Too), to the local military chief and said the Lord had spoken to them and they would save the Karen people.

A new rebel group called God’s Army of the Holy Mountain, or God’s Army for short, was formed on the border of Burma and Thailand under the nominal leadership of the Htoo twins.

Various legends claimed that the brothers had numerous magical powers, including invulnerability to bullets and mines, and that they could hand out magical bullets. Supposedly, they could kill by pointing a rifle at the ground and concentrating. One tale claims that Johnny turned himself into an old man and back when bathing in a river. The legend of the boys was embraced by locals who viewed the existing Karen National Union as corrupt and ineffective.
