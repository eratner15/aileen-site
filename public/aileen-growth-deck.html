Right Now

Phase 1: Money Foundation.
Days 1-7.

Nothing else happens until the revenue infrastructure is built. Seven days of pure monetization setup.

  • 1
    Days 1-2: Audit every existing post for affiliate links.
    Open each post. Run the monetization audit. Add ShopMy links (fashion/beauty/home, 10-25% commission) and Amazon links (kitchen/books/travel, 1-4%). Target: 8-10 posts per day. Zero free mentions.
  • 2
    Days 3-4: Write and publish 5 "Shop the Edit" posts.
    These are your money pages: Spring Capsule Wardrobe, Kitchen Essentials, Beauty Shelf, Dinner Party Guide, Travel Packing List. 10-15 affiliate links each. All pre-loaded in your content calendar.
  • 3
    Days 5-6: ShopMy storefront overhaul + brand DMs.
    Create 5 collections in ShopMy (Style, Beauty, Kitchen, Home, Travel). Start sending 10 brand DMs per day — templates are in your system. Track every DM in a spreadsheet.
  • 4
    Day 7: Ad revenue setup + site fixes.
    Apply to Mediavine Journey (or Google AdSense as backup). Fix nav, broken widgets, missing email opt-ins. Ads should be earning something by end of week 1.
  • 5
    Then: 2 posts per day, every day.
    Morning post published by 8 AM. Afternoon post by 1 PM. Each gets 3 Pinterest pins + Instagram Story the same day. This is the engine. 14 posts per week. No exceptions.
  • 6
    Week 5: Launch "The AILEEN Letter" on Substack.
    Weekly newsletter. Personal note + this week's posts + one product find + "currently" list + shop section. Add signup form to every page. Create a free lead magnet PDF.
